# Gluttons Garage: Brand Notes (from the real photos)

## 1. The logo

**Warning:** `logo-57.jpg` is **not** the café's logo. It's a generic flat icon (a blue serving cloche and a purple martini glass with slate outlines), probably a placeholder from a listing site. Don't use it.

The **real logo** shows up as a watermark on the professional studio food shots (photo-26, 28–30, 32–34, 37, food-41…49) and as a painted wall mural (yappe-689582, -689592, -689593). There is no clean standalone file of it in `raw/`; a designer should ask the owner for a vector, or redraw it from photo-28 or photo-37, which are the cleanest watermarks.

- **Wordmark**
  - "GLUTTONS" is set in all caps in a heavy, retro, Victorian/western-style display serif with flared, bracketed serifs, similar to Cooper Black or "Rye"/"Bevan".
  - The "G" has a swash tail that curls left under the word, and the letters bounce slightly.
  - "garage" is lowercase in a smaller rounded geometric sans (like Nunito or Quicksand Bold). It sits centred under "GLUTTONS" and is tucked to the right.
- **Emblem**
  - A circular ring of small line and solid icons surrounds the wordmark.
  - Above it: a burger bun, a baguette, a beer/juice glass, music notes, an orange slice and sparkles.
  - Below it: chilli, sausage, wine glass, fork, noodle bowl and flowers.
  - The ring reads as "food + drinks + music".
- **Colours (watermark version)**
  - Wordmark: deep maroon-brown, about **#5A1F1B**.
  - Icon ring: warm orange, about **#F08A1C** (a range of roughly #EE8A1E to #F39A2B).
  - Background: white.
- **Mural version**
  - The same lockup in monochrome navy-black (#1E2230) on white.
  - It sits inside a scalloped, zig-zag-edged mandala/rosette that is split vertically half black and half white.
  - Brush-script "Fusion" and "Happens" flank it.
- **Exterior sign (photo-23, yappe-109699)**
  - "GLUTTONS garage" in the same wordmark style, made of white 3D letters (warm-lit at night).
  - It's on a black fascia framed by olive-khaki **Jeep grille cut-outs with round headlamps and wiper arms**. This is the "garage" motif.

## 2. Interior style and vibe

The style is **industrial "garage" café with playful, youthful fusion energy.** It is casual and a hangout spot for students and young professionals, not upscale.

- **Garage/machine motifs:**
  - A big black-and-white **gear/cog wall mural**.
  - **Bicycle-wheel pendant lights** hung inside black wire-mesh ceiling frames.
  - Caged/slatted metal pendant lamps.
  - Exposed red-oxide conduit pipes.
  - A **vintage motorcycle** displayed at the entrance.
- **Floor:** a classic **black-and-cream checkerboard** throughout. It is the strongest visual signature.
- **Seating:**
  - Brown/rust **velvet tub chairs** with bentwood arms.
  - Glass-top tables.
  - Chunky **rough-hewn timber benches and tables**.
  - Metal café chairs in khaki.
- **Surfaces:**
  - Exposed **terracotta brick** walls.
  - Striped wood-veneer bulkheads.
  - Counters clad in multicolour **reclaimed-wood planks** (teal, grey and tan).
  - Grey concrete pillars.
- **Art:**
  - Hand-lettered chalk-style murals ("Filling Station" drinks wall).
  - A sketched caricature figure.
  - A "Music / Theatre / Story Telling / Stand-up Comedy / Art Exhibition" mural. The space doubles as an events and open-mic venue.
- **Food styling:** pro shots use plain white crockery (spoon/pan-shaped platters, divided trays, wide-rim bowls). The best lifestyle shots use a grey-brown wood tabletop.

## 4. Signage text seen

- "GLUTTONS garage" on the main fascia, day and night.
- "Fusion Happens" brush script on the logo mural.
- The entrance banner reads "…NGLE ☐ TAKEN ☐ HUNGRY ☑", a playful "single / taken / hungry" checkbox joke.
- "OPEN" hanging tags.
- "FILLING STATION" on the drinks counter pillar. The wall mural lists: Mocktails, Freak Shakes, Smoothie, Coffee, Ice Tea, Cold Pressed Juices, Thirsty?, Chai, Soda, Frappe.
- "Music · Theatre · Story Telling · Stand-up Comedy · Art Exhibition" mural.
- "SELF ORDER" and "PULL" door signs; Paytm and PhonePe payment stickers.
- Dessert-case labels: Caramel Custard, Lotus Biscoff, Cheese Cake, Biscoff Cheese Cake, Tiramisu, Choco Lava, Hazelnut Browny, Brownie Sandwich, White Chocolate Blondies, Muffins, Belgium / Truffle / Swiss / Hazelnut / Mocha / Caramel Chocolate, Caramel Slice, Assorted Cupcake.
- **Co-located brands** share the frontage: **Kaloreez**, **Just Not Shakes** ("Healthy Juices · Freaky Shakes", printed on the drink bottles) and **Junktion77**. A designer should confirm with the owner whether they belong to the same group before showing them on the site.

## 5. Recommended brand palette

This palette comes from the real logo (maroon and orange) and the interior (checkerboard, rust velvet, brick, timber).

| Role | Name | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary / wordmark | Glutton Maroon | **#5A1F1B** |
| Accent / icons, CTAs | Garage Orange | **#F08A1C** |
| Dark base / text | Checker Black | **#1C1A19** |
| Light base / backgrounds | Checker Cream | **#F3EBDD** |
| Warm secondary | Rust Velvet (brick/chairs) | **#9A4A30** |
| Natural support | Timber Brown | **#8A5A2E** |
| Cool counterpoint (sparingly) | Reclaimed Teal | **#5E8A86** |

**Type suggestion:**
- Headings: a retro flared serif in the spirit of the wordmark, e.g. *Bevan*, *Rye* or *Cooper*-style; use it sparingly.
- Sub-heads: a rounded sans echoing "garage", e.g. *Nunito* or *Quicksand*.
- Accents: a brush script for "Fusion Happens"-style lines.
- Motifs: checkerboard bands, gear outlines, and the food-icon ring.

**Photo caveats:**
- Most of the professional studio food shots have the logo **watermarked in the top-left corner**. Crop it out or ask for the originals.
- About 15 files are exact duplicates (see `use_for: "skip"` notes in photos.json).
- The diner and staff photos show identifiable faces. Get consent before using them.
